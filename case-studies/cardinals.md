# Cardinals — Year 1 Game Development

**DigiPen GAM150 · January–April 2026 · Team Orbital Candy (five members)**

A 2D platformer where players manipulate gravity to traverse obstacles, avoid hazards and reach the exit.

[Original repository](https://github.com/JeromeIsntDev/GAM150_Cardinals) · [Gameplay video](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/04ae0e83-f9fa-4dec-99e4-90578be4c729)

## Problem and constraints

Create a platformer built around gravity manipulation within a semester-long collaborative studio project.

## Development and testing

The team combined gameplay programming, debugging and version control. The gameplay video demonstrates the core mechanic and traversal loop; it is not a formal test report.
