# Trivio — Classroom Engagement Prototype

**SIT · UDE2222 · Trimester 3 AY2025/26 · Five-member team**

An interactive lecture companion designed to help students sustain attention and recover from attention lapses.

[Original repository](https://github.com/tingjs05/Trivio) · [Demo](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/5c150ba3-9297-40c8-aaf7-96e3cfab50a5) · [Figma](https://www.figma.com/design/gKd2AGl6Xi4P1bZcqgDi7R/Trivio)

## Problem and constraints

Fast-paced university lectures can leave students struggling to recover missed concepts. The design needed to serve both students and educators without disrupting teaching or increasing anxiety.

## What we tested

The team researched student and educator needs, explored interface concepts and gathered feedback on low-fidelity prototypes before refining a mid-fidelity prototype.

## What failed

Initial feedback identified distracting collectible pop-ups, unclear student/professor interface separation and concerns about competitive pressure.

## What changed

The team refined separate student and educator views, connected short attention checks (Pulses) to lecture content, and explored private recovery through Micro-Recaps.

## Outcome

The project progressed from Figma to an interactive web prototype, demonstrating collaborative research, ideation and iterative design. No measured learning-outcome improvement or production deployment is claimed.

## Run locally

The upstream README documents `pnpm install` and `pnpm dev` (pnpm 9). See the original repository for prerequisites. A public browser deployment has not been verified.
